About UI Online Typing Test

UI Online Typing Test is an online typing practice platform designed for students, beginners and professionals.

Users can take typing tests, practice different paragraphs, play typing games and check their typing performance.

The main goal is to help users improve their typing speed while maintaining good accuracy.
